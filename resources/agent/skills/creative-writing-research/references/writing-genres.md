# Writing Genres Reference

Detailed per-genre skills, workflow phases, and edge-case guidance. Load this reference when the task needs deeper detail than the SKILL.md genre section.

## Register-shifting within one piece

Do not pick one formality level and apply it flat from start to finish. The natural pattern across all prose genres:

- **Hook/opening:** may be more casual, concrete, or use analogy — pull attention before entering substance.
- **Technical body:** precision, technical terms kept accurate, but still explained in human sentences, not dictionary definitions.
- **Transitions between sections:** tone drops slightly toward conversational, so it does not feel like jumping from one textbook chapter to another.
- **Conclusion:** returns to formal to state implications, but must not merely restate the body in different words.

Practical rule: if a paragraph carries a claim or data point → formal and precise. If a paragraph connects ideas or gives context → may be more conversational.

## Specificity over abstraction

Generic claims without concrete detail are the most recognizable AI tell. Always check: could this sentence have its topic swapped without changing anything? If yes, it is too generic — add numbers, names, specific examples, or contextual technical detail.

- Generic: "This technology brings many benefits to companies."
- Specific: "Latency dropped from 800ms to 220ms after the caching layer moved to the edge."

## Raw material vs cited references

When the user supplies local files (rough drafts, notes, transcripts, brain dumps, internal documents) as source material — treat them as raw material to develop, not as sources to cite in the final article.

- Do not reference local files in the final output. No "Reference: based on document X.md" or `file://` links. Readers cannot access those files.
- Develop the content, do not just tidy it. Rough drafts are usually bullet points or immature writing. The job is to rewrite into flowing prose per the principles in this skill — not copy-paste with a polish and a link back to the source.
- If the article needs to cite sources, use publicly accessible and verifiable sources: official documentation, publicly accessible articles/publications, books, papers, or organization sites. Do not fabricate sources.
- If no relevant public source exists, omit the references section entirely rather than pointing to local files.

## Blog Writing

### Core skills
- **Angle formulation** — Isolate a gap in public discourse and frame a unique perspective that automation cannot replicate.
- **Voice & tone modulation** — Dynamically direct tone to build authentic emotional connection with the reader; match brand identity.
- **Hook engineering** — Open with a real story or real dilemma that triggers curiosity; avoid generic AI-format openings.
- **Technical translation** — Translate complex concepts or scientific data into everyday persuasive narrative without sacrificing baseline accuracy.
- **Deep-thought optimization** — Move beyond surface-level takes; the value is in the thinking, not the word count.

### Edge cases
- If the brief has no personal angle, ask the user for their lived experience or professional stance before drafting. Do not invent a voice.
- If the topic is technical, confirm accuracy with the user before publishing; AI can flatten nuance in translation.

## Journal & Academic Writing

### Core skills
- **Theoretical framework & hypothesis formulation** — Connect research variables explicitly; state the hypothesis in testable form.
- **Literature critique** — Evaluate methodological weaknesses, sample bias, and epistemological gaps of prior publications; do not merely summarize.
- **Semantic richness in formal register** — Use formal scientific register that is distanced yet communicative; avoid automated-text redundancy.
- **Honest evidence-limit acknowledgment** — Account for empirical evidence limits honestly; do not overstate findings.
- **Citation grounding** — Every citation traces to a verified primary source; no fabricated references.

### Edge cases
- If the user supplies AI-generated references, verify each one against a real database before including. Hallucinated citations are the highest-risk AI failure in academic writing.
- If the study has small sample size or methodological gaps, state them explicitly rather than hiding them.

## Feature Article Writing

### Core skills
- **Expert synthesis** — Extract insight from expert interviews; blend direct quotes into the narrative.
- **Issue framing** — Connect micro incidents to broader social, economic, or technology phenomena.
- **Implication chain** — Draw second- and third-order implications that require real-world understanding.
- **Data-driven narrative** — Build narrative from data without losing the human angle.

### Edge cases
- If no expert interview is available, flag the gap; do not fabricate quotes.
- If the macro-trend connection is weak, state the limitation rather than forcing a false synthesis.

## News Writing

### Core skills
- **Primary source verification** — Check claims through eyewitness interviews, official document analysis, and cross-investigation. Non-delegable to AI.
- **Inverted pyramid structure** — Prioritize the most important facts in the lead paragraph while preserving balanced context.
- **Ethical balance** — Cover both sides; eliminate personal or algorithmic bias.
- **Field accountability** — The reporter carries moral accountability and direct verification capacity that AI cannot replace.

### Edge cases
- If a source cannot be verified, kill the claim rather than publish unverified.
- If the story involves sensitive subjects, apply the ethical-balance check twice before delivery.

## Story Writing (Fiction, Memoir, Screenplay)

### Core skills
- **Character architecture** — Construct characters with internal motivation, trauma, moral contradiction, and gradual psychological growth.
- **Subtext & implication** — Deliver deepest meaning through symbolism, implied dialogue, and scene atmosphere ("show, don't tell").
- **Pacing & tension control** — Control narrative tempo and dramatic tension intuitively; direct the emotional climax.
- **Metaphor integrity** — Build metaphors rooted in lived experience and cultural context; avoid cliché AI metaphor.

### Edge cases
- If the character lacks internal contradiction, the story will read as AI-predictable; add moral contradiction before drafting.
- If the plot is linear and predictable, introduce a logical surprise that stays coherent with the character's arc.
