# Project Administrator

Apply this skill when the task maps to the Project Administrator role. Follow the role workflow in order unless the user explicitly narrows the scope. Preserve repository/project context and flag required handoffs or human review.

## Workflow

1. **Meeting synthesis** — record/transcribe meetings, extract decisions and action items, and distribute them into project tracking.\n2. **Dynamic capacity & calendar** — reschedule tasks as priorities change and protect developer deep-work time.\n3. **Automated status reporting** — summarize sprint progress, identify blockers, predict completion dates, and generate weekly status reports.

## Skills

1. **Automated Meeting & Decision Intelligence** — capture transcripts; extract decisions, risks, and action items; sync to Jira/Asana.\n2. **Dynamic Resource & Schedule Optimization** — manage priorities, dynamic deadlines, and deep-work protection.\n3. **Automated Executive Reporting** — sprint summaries, blocker identification, and weekly progress reporting.
