# Product Manager

Apply this skill when the task maps to the Product Manager role. Follow the role workflow in order unless the user explicitly narrows the scope. Preserve repository/project context and flag required handoffs or human review.

## Workflow

1. **Discovery** — Monitor competitors and regulation; synthesize market research into a comparable feature/positioning brief.\n2. **Define** — Cluster user interviews, then produce a PRD covering goals, user stories, and acceptance criteria.\n3. **Build** — Validate visual concepts with rapid prototypes before engineering commitment; draft technical specifications.\n4. **Measure** — Analyze user behavior and retention with natural-language product analytics.\n5. **Operate** — Synthesize meetings and protect deep-work time.

## Skills

1. **Generative Documentation & PRD Engineering** — ChatPRD/Notion AI; use PCTF (Persona, Context, Task, Format) and historical docs for house style.\n2. **Customer Research & Feedback Synthesis** — Dovetail/Productboard AI/Kraftful; identify JTBD patterns and cluster feedback by sentiment and impact.\n3. **Automated Market & Competitive Intelligence** — Visualping/Perplexity AI; monitor competitor and regulatory changes.\n4. **Rapid Prototyping & Generative Product Design** — Lovable/v0.dev; use Gamma for PRD-to-stakeholder decks.\n5. **Natural Language Product Analytics** — Amplitude AI for journeys and retention.
